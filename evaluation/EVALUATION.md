## Method

- **Question set:** `evaluation/eval_questions.csv` — 26 questions: factual, methodology, results, limitations, cross-paper comparison, synthesis, figure (paper figure + uploaded image), unanswerable, prompt injection.
- **Ground truth:** each expected keyword is checked to occur in the expected paper's indexed text before scoring (all passed).
- **Path under test:** `backend.dispatch.route_question` — the same router `POST /ask` uses.
- **Automatic checks:** route chosen, expected paper(s) cited, expected facts present in the answer, abstention on unanswerable questions, no prompt leak / injected instruction ignored, and the share of claims the evidence check marks *supported* (factual types, `verify=True`).
- **Limits of the method:** keyword checks confirm the key fact is present, not that the whole answer is correct; comparison and synthesis are scored on routing and attribution only (their prose quality needs human review).

## Known limitations

- Small set (26 questions) built from the papers' own text; it is a smoke-level benchmark, not a statistically robust accuracy estimate.
- Keyword scoring can pass an answer that also contains errors, and can fail a correct answer that paraphrases a number (e.g. “six” vs “6” is handled, other paraphrases may not be).
- Figure descriptions in the index are AI-generated; answers that rely on them are marked as such in their sources.
- Vector-drawn figures are not extracted by the PDF figure extractor, so questions about them fall back to captions and surrounding text.
- Latency includes Gemini API calls and CPU reranking; comparison/synthesis are slower because they retrieve per paper and per dimension.
